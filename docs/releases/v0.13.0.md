# kasane-pdf v0.13.0

## Overview

0.13.0 draws dense tiling patterns from a periodic texture, draws CFF
glyphs that carry the deprecated `dotsection` hint, redacts pages that
set a soft mask away from the rects, and fixes security issues in
redaction.

- **Tiling patterns**: fills of more than 64 tiles are drawn from one
  period of the tiling rendered once at up to 8 pixels per device pixel
  and averaged over each device pixel: thin lines in small cells keep
  their coverage, tiles meet without seams and do not drift, and cells
  whose `/BBox` exceeds the step (including TikZ's rounding) no longer
  run into `max_pattern_tiles`. Lines and glyph outlines of width 0
  stay one device pixel wide, also in a pattern colour, and pattern
  strokes take the stroking alpha.
- **CFF fonts**: glyphs whose charstrings carry `dotsection` (Type 1
  fonts converted to CFF: dots and accents) are drawn; they were empty.
  The program is rewritten in memory within an allocation budget, or
  left as it was.
- **Redaction**: a page that sets a soft mask with `gs` is redacted when
  the mask draws nothing that meets a rect; it was refused whatever the
  mask.
- **Text extraction**: a `/ToUnicode` CMap that defines a code twice
  gives it its later definition.

MSRV stays 1.92. No public signature changes (`cargo semver-checks`
against 0.12.0), but behaviour callers can observe does, hence the
minor version ([`api-stability.md`](../api-stability.md)): pages with
dense tiling patterns, CFF glyphs with `dotsection` or pattern-coloured
strokes render differently; extracted text follows the later definition
of a twice-defined ToUnicode code; some redactions succeed that were
refused (soft masks), and some are refused that succeeded (see
*Security*).

## Security

Upgrading is recommended for every application that uses `redact`.

- [GHSA-pp5g-3qw2-h45w](https://github.com/akam1o/kasane-pdf/security/advisories/GHSA-pp5g-3qw2-h45w)
  (Medium, 0.6.0–0.12.0): content an annotation's appearance draws
  beyond its `/BBox`, inside a rect while the annotation's `/Rect` is
  clear of it, was kept. Such annotations now meet the rect (removed or
  refused); appearances that cannot be bounded are refused.
- [GHSA-844w-wgrm-c4q8](https://github.com/akam1o/kasane-pdf/security/advisories/GHSA-844w-wgrm-c4q8)
  (Low, 0.6.0–0.12.0): a shading clipped away from the rects was kept
  with its geometry inside one. Its geometry is now checked (and that of
  shading patterns used as a colour); axial shadings keep their axis and
  function, as the safety model now states.

The 0.13.0 package carries the fix; its CHANGELOG and release notes
held only a general statement until the advisories were published, and
the advisories' regression tests were added on `main` afterwards.

## Known limitations

- `/TilingType 1` (constant spacing) is not honoured: ISO 32000 asks
  for its cells to be a whole number of device pixels apart; kasane
  places every pattern's cells at their exact spacing, as MuPDF does
  ([`limitations.md`](../limitations.md)). Poppler draws such a pattern
  whose `/BBox` equals its step from a whole-pixel tile, so its cells
  can drift from kasane's by a fraction of a pixel per step.
- Because of that, and of Poppler's snapping of single-rectangle fills
  to whole pixels, two pages of the compatibility corpus move away from
  Poppler while moving towards MuPDF and the exact coverage
  (`issue16038` page 1, `mad_kp` 18.11661 → 19.59712 at the 72 dpi grid
  of 300 dpi renders; `verapdf_verapdf-test-suite-6-2-3-3-t01-fail-o`
  page 1, content SSIM 0.9551 → 0.9333 at 96 dpi). The regression gate
  accepts exactly these two measures of these two pages, up to these
  values, in the Linux and macOS reference environments it was reviewed
  in (`compat/reviewed-differences.json`); every other measure, page and
  environment is gated as before, and the baselines are unchanged.
- A rotated or skewed tiling is averaged over each device pixel in
  2 × 2 parts (edge pixels can differ by a few levels from the exact
  footprint); stroke adjustment is not applied inside the texture.

## Verification

The release gate of [`docs/releasing.md`](../releasing.md) runs on the
release commit before it is published: the fast and full CI gates
(MSRV, semver-checks, the redaction model at 10,000 seeds), CodeQL with
no unreviewed result, and the local verification (the redaction corpus
in four option sets, the 511-file compatibility corpus on Linux and
macOS, the external renderers, long fuzzing). The commit and the
results are recorded with the GitHub Release.

## Corpus

The 511-file compatibility corpus is one evaluation set, not a success
rate. Against 0.12.0's main on the Linux reference: 1148 pages compared,
seven more similar to Poppler by SSIM (the tiling and CFF fixes), one
less similar within the gate's threshold, two files that failed to
render now render; text F1 unchanged; no crash or timeout.
