# kasane-pdf v0.6.1

## Overview

0.6.1 is a security fix release for `redact`, introduced in 0.6.0. It
contains two fixes and their tests; nothing else changes. No public API
changes (`cargo semver-checks` against 0.6.0: no semver update
required). MSRV stays 1.92. Upgrading is recommended for every
application that uses `redact`.

## Security

### Content hidden by a clip inside a rect was kept (GHSA-x455-j82p-6jmv)

- *What:* `redact` decided whether content met a rect inside the
  current clip. Text, images and paths that a clip kept from showing
  inside every rect were not hits and stayed in the output, and text in
  a non-embedded font under a rectangular clip clear of the rects was
  accepted. A clip hides ink, not data.
- *Impact:* a successful redaction could keep, at the position of a
  rect, text that extraction and selection still find there (with
  `pdftotext`, for example), image samples and path coordinates —
  invisible on the page because of the clip, present in the file.
- *Fixed:* in redaction mode text, images and paths are hits wherever
  they meet a rect, clipped or not, and are removed, zeroed or cut; the
  non-embedded exception is withdrawn (such text is refused). Content
  under a clip that is not a rectangle is no longer refused for that
  reason. The one deliberate exception is a shading (`sh`), which fills
  the clip and is still accepted when that clip is a rectangle meeting
  no rect (its resource is kept as it is).

### Strongly anisotropic text could be kept where it is drawn inside a rect (GHSA-frr2-h56x-mr4h)

- *What:* under a text matrix stretching one axis far more than the
  other (0.1 × 10 measured), Poppler draws glyphs away from where their
  outlines lie; `redact` placed the code by its outline, outside a
  rect, while the reader drew it inside — also when the CTM cancelled
  the stretch in the product.
- *Fixed:* `redact` refuses text whose text matrix, CTM or their
  product stretches one axis over 16 times the other (agreement was
  measured up to 16:1).

Both were measured on 0.6.0 (the tests added here fail there); `redact`
is not in any earlier release. The diagnosis and the `remove_*`
editors are unchanged.

## Behaviour changes

- More content inside the rects is removed: what a clip hid there. As
  for any glyph meeting a rect, a glyph is removed whole, so a glyph
  whose clipped-away part meets a rect also loses its visible part
  outside it.
- New refusals: non-embedded text under a clip clear of the rects
  (accepted in 0.6.0), and text under a text matrix or CTM stretched
  over 16:1.
