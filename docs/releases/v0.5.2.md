# kasane-pdf v0.5.2

## Overview

0.5.2 is a security fix release. The content walker behind
`diagnose_redaction` and the experimental content editors composed
nested transforms in the wrong order, so content drawn under more than
one transform was located at the wrong coordinates. Nothing else
changes: this release is 0.5.1 plus that fix and its tests.

No public API changes (`cargo semver-checks` against 0.5.1: no semver
update required). MSRV stays 1.92. Upgrading is recommended for every
application that uses `diagnose_redaction` or the `remove_*` editors.

## Security

**Content under nested transforms was located at the wrong
coordinates (GHSA-2337-4h5g-jqwx).**

- *What:* the walker composed a nested `cm`, and a Form XObject's
  `/Matrix`, as CTM × M instead of M × CTM (ISO 32000-2 §8.4.4,
  §8.10.1). A single transform was located correctly; content drawn
  under two or more — common in files written by browsers and office
  software — was not.
- *Affected:* `diagnose_redaction` (CLI `preflight-redaction`) and the
  experimental editors `remove_text`, `remove_image_region`,
  `remove_region`, `remove_regions`,
  `remove_regions_and_document_metadata` (CLI `experimental-remove-*`).
  Rendering and text extraction compose transforms correctly and are
  not affected. Measured on 0.5.1; the same code is present unchanged
  in every release from 0.1.0.
- *Impact:* for content under nested transforms, the editors could
  leave content inside the caller's rectangles in place while returning
  `Ok` (with lower counts than expected, possibly 0), and could edit
  content outside them; `diagnose_redaction` could report no target in
  a rectangle that contains painted text or images, with no blocker or
  uncertainty. A caller that relied on these APIs to take information
  out of a region before sharing a document could share it with that
  information still visible and extractable. These APIs are documented
  as content editors and as a diagnosis without a "safe" verdict, not
  as redaction tools (`docs/security.md`); that has not changed.
- *Fixed in 0.5.2:* transforms compose as M × CTM, as the renderer
  composes them. New tests: an image under two `cm` operators, a form's
  `/Matrix` under a `cm`.
- *Recommendation:* upgrade, and re-check documents edited or
  diagnosed with an earlier version if they draw content under nested
  transforms. Keep verifying edited output independently (render it,
  extract its text with another tool), as the documentation advises.

## Behaviour changes

Content under nested transforms is located — and edited — where it is
drawn. Results for content under a single transform are unchanged.

## Known limitations

As in 0.5.1. The `remove_*` editors and `diagnose_redaction` are not
redaction tools: they report what they changed or found, not that
information is gone from the document (`docs/security.md`).
