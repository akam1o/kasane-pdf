# kasane-pdf v0.12.0

## Overview

0.12.0 draws non-embedded Courier and Courier New with a Courier design,
and checks every font redaction embeds.

- **Kasane Courier**: non-embedded Courier and Courier New (a Standard
  14 name or its alias, every style) draw with the bundled Kasane
  Courier — Courier Prime 3.018 with every character it lacks taken
  from Liberation Mono 2.1.5 — instead of Liberation Mono
  ([`fonts/kasane-courier/README.md`](../../fonts/kasane-courier/README.md)).
  Other fixed-pitch fonts keep Liberation Mono.
- **Redaction**: with `RedactOptions::embed_substitute_fonts`, Courier
  text is embedded as Kasane Courier, and the check of the rewritten
  document covers every font it embedded, whatever its name.

MSRV stays 1.92. No public signature changes (`cargo semver-checks`
against 0.11.0: no update required), but behaviour callers can observe
does, hence the minor version
([`api-stability.md`](../api-stability.md)):
- pages with non-embedded Courier text render differently, and their
  glyph bounds follow the new outlines — so does which glyphs a
  redaction rect near their edge touches; advances and extracted text
  are unchanged, and every character drawn before is still drawn;
- `RenderedPage::font_substitutions()` names `KasaneCourier-*` faces
  for them (the substitution kind is unchanged);
- `embed_substitute_fonts` embeds Kasane Courier, not Liberation Mono,
  for Courier text.

## Kasane Courier

How a reader draws a non-embedded Courier depends on the reader and on
the substitute fonts it has; kasane still reports the substitution as
one. Kasane Courier is not Adobe's Courier. Compared with Liberation
Mono, the previous substitute, it was measured during development as
closer to these references
([`courier-reference.md`](../courier-reference.md)):

- **Poppler drawing Courier with Nimbus Mono PS** (the nightly's
  reference, E1), on the 511-file compatibility corpus — one evaluation
  set, not a success rate: 8 pages more similar by SSIM, none less;
  page classes and text F1 unchanged. Poppler draws no page of 24 of
  the files, so they have no reference image and are not covered by
  this comparison.
- **Synthetic cases** (25, at 96 and 150 dpi: 23 in Courier or Courier
  New and two controls, another fixed-pitch font and Helvetica, that
  draw identically before and after), SSIM of the text area,
  differences within 0.001 counted as the same. Of the 23:
  - Poppler with Nimbus Mono PS: 22 more similar, one less — an invalid
    font name, where the references disagree with each other;
  - MuPDF: 23 more similar;
  - pdf.js: 23 more similar at 96 dpi; at 150 dpi 22, and one the same.

Against Poppler drawing Courier with Liberation Mono — the reference
then draws the previous substitute itself — it is less similar: the
same 8 corpus pages and all 23 synthetic Courier cases.

The four faces (749,104 bytes, about 0.75 MB, in total) are compiled
into the library and shipped in the crate. An output of
`embed_substitute_fonts` carries each face it embeds once; how much it
grows depends on the faces embedded and their compression. The fonts
are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
([`fonts/kasane-courier/LICENSE`](../../fonts/kasane-courier/LICENSE),
`NOTICE`); `scripts/build-courier-fallback.py` rebuilds them from
pinned inputs, and a lock (`scripts/kasane-courier.sha256`) checks
them.

## Fixes

- `embed_substitute_fonts`: the check of the rewritten document compares
  every font it embedded with what was written (program bytes, encoding,
  advances); it skipped fonts not named `Liberation…`. Every substitute
  in a released version was one, so no released output was affected.

## Verification

The release gate of [`docs/releasing.md`](../releasing.md) runs on the
release commit before it is published: the fast and full CI gates,
CodeQL with no unreviewed result, the nightly corpus on Linux and the
local verification (the redaction corpus in four option sets, the
511-file compatibility corpus, the external renderers, long fuzzing and
the redaction model at 10,000 seeds). Courier redactions are read back
by Poppler, MuPDF and pdf.js, including a rect at the edge of a glyph
and deliberately broken outputs. The commit and the results are
recorded with the GitHub Release.
